# Superstore Sales Analytics Dashboard (Tableau)

# Overview
This Tableau dashboard provides a detailed analysis of sales data from a fictional retail chain named Superstore, USA. The visualizations give insights into key performance indicators like sales, profit margins, and return rates across various categories, sub-categories, regions, and customer segments.

# Key Features
Sales by Category:
Visualizes the proportion of total sales contributed by Technology (40.65%), Furniture (34.60%), and Office Supplies (24.75%).

Sales by Sub-Category:
Highlights top-performing sub-categories such as Phones, Chairs, and Copiers with respective percentage contributions.

# KPI Summary:

Total Sales: ₹0.10M

Profit Margin: 11.39%

Return Rate: 5.91%

Sales by State (Map):
Interactive map showcasing sales distribution across states like Texas, Michigan, Indiana, etc.

Sales by Segment:
Donut chart splitting sales among Consumer (48.56%), Corporate (27.34%), and Home Office (24.08%) segments.

Top 5 Products:
Lists the highest-selling products, e.g.,

Fellowes PB500 Electric Punch

HON 5400 Series Task Chairs

Sales Trend (Time Series):
Line graph showing sales trends over months in 2015, indicating growth with noticeable spikes around October–November.

# Filters Panel (Interactivity):

Choose Metric: Sales, Profit, or Order Count

Filter by Region: Central, East, South, West

Select Year of Order: (in this case, 2015)

# Conclusion
This Tableau dashboard offers a comprehensive view of Superstore’s sales performance across products, regions, and customer segments. By visualizing key metrics like profit margin, return rate, and sales trends, it helps identify:

High-performing products and categories

Sales growth patterns over time

Regional strengths and weaknesses

Customer segments driving the most revenue

This analysis enables data-driven decision-making for inventory planning, marketing strategies, and regional focus, ultimately improving business performance and customer satisfaction.
